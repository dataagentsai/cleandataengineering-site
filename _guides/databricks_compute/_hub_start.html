People mix up four separate questions. Keep them apart and most of the confusion goes away.

QuestionPossible answersWhere it is explained
Who owns the machines?You (classic) or Databricks (serverless)Two planes
What kind of work runs on it?Notebooks and code (clusters) or SQL only (SQL warehouse)The family, warehouses and serverless
How fast does it run the work?Plain Spark engine or Photon enginePhoton
How long does it live?Stays up for a team (interactive) or exists for one run (job)Classic clusters

"SQL Warehouse", "Serverless" and "Photon" are not three competing things. A SQL warehouse is a type of compute. Serverless is who owns the machines. Photon is the engine inside. A serverless SQL warehouse is all three at once.
