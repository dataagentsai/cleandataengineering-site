Databricks changes compute features often, and I wrote this from knowledge rather than a live docs check. These are the places I would verify before relying on a number or a limit:

  • Exact start-up times and the DBU price multipliers for Photon, Pro and serverless.
  • Which Spark cache calls and which languages serverless notebooks and jobs support today.
  • Whether disk cache is on by default for a given instance type on your cloud.
  • Whether the serverless-specific Predictive IO and workload management features differ for notebooks and jobs.
  • Remote result cache length (I wrote up to 24 hours).
  • Every dollar rate and the DBU-per-size table in the billing page (illustrative only), and the exact SKU names.
  • Serverless budget policy details and which serverless products they cover, and exact system table names, columns and retention.
  • Whether your contract discount is reflected anywhere in system tables.
  • Lakebase synced-table behaviour and pricing, and whether system tables can be read as a stream.
  • Cloud cross-region and cross-zone transfer rates for your provider, and the current UNDROP window.
  • Delta Sharing egress options (such as replicas or zero-egress storage) for your cloud.

The core structure, though, is stable: driver plus workers, control plane versus compute plane, Photon as a swap-in engine, and the cache layers from RAM to SSD to cloud storage.
