Prototype vs product

A prototype proves value on real data; a product survives every day after.

What it is

A prototype is fast and narrow, built in days to show the idea works on the customer's real data. A product adds the harness: security, tests, monitoring, evaluation, cost limits, hand-over. Be clear which one you are building.

Why it matters

Mixing them up either kills momentum or ships something fragile.

An example

Week 1: a notebook on 200 real claims shows 85% field accuracy. Weeks 2–8: the same logic, hardened and deployed.

The common mistake

Putting the demo notebook into production.

Where you meet it on each platform

The idea stays the same; only the product name changes.
