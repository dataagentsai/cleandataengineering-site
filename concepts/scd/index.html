Slowly changing dimensions

How to record that a customer moved city, without rewriting history.

What it is

Descriptions change over time. Type 1 overwrites the old value, so history shows the new one. Type 2 adds a new row with valid-from and valid-to dates, so last year's sales stay with last year's city.

See it happen: Problem Lab: SCD Type 2 pitfalls, the naive fix and the right one (simulation), recorded from a real run.

Why it matters

Without it, moving one customer quietly changes last year's regional sales report.

An example

Asha moves from Pune to Sydney: her old row is closed on 15 Nov, a new Sydney row opens, and past orders still count for Pune.

The common mistake

Using Type 1 everywhere, then being asked why last year's numbers changed.

Where you meet it on each platform

The idea stays the same; only the product name changes.
