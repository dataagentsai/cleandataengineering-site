Glossary

All concepts, A to Z

  • Agree the measure of success first: Decide with the customer how you will know it worked, before you build.
  • APIs and contracts: A promised way for programs to talk, which you change carefully.
  • Attention and the transformer: Each word looks at every other word to decide what matters.
  • Backfills and reprocessing: Re-run a pipeline for past dates, safely.
  • Batch vs online prediction: Score everything on a schedule, or one request at a time as it comes.
  • Batch vs streaming: Process data in scheduled chunks, or event by event as it happens.
  • Benchmarks and contamination: Public scores are a starting point; your own tests decide.
  • Caching: Keep a copy of a result so you don't compute it again.
  • Change data capture (CDC): Copy only what changed in a database, by reading its change log.

  • Chunking: How you cut documents into pieces decides what search can find.

  • Data map and owners: Where the customer's data lives, what shape it is in, and who can say yes.
  • Data quality dimensions: Six ways data goes wrong, and a check for each.
