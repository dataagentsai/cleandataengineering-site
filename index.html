Research and open standards

Clean AI Engineering

The interesting failures are almost never in the model. They are in the scaffolding around it: the context assembled wrongly, the retry that charged the customer twice, the loop that never stopped. That scaffolding can be specified, built and tested by ordinary means. These specifications are published in the open, versioned and machine-readable.

AI Assurance Catalog

AAC · what must be TRUE

Test obligations for AI applications, by architecture type, with crosswalks to external frameworks.

Read the catalogGitHub

AI Harness Catalog

AHC · what must EXIST

Harness capabilities across sixteen layers and ten archetypes. Observability and evals are two of sixteen layers, and that is the point.
