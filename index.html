Mindset

The lion mindset

The best way to survive AI is not to try to survive. A lion doesn't spend the day hiding from the jungle; it moves first. Be the driver, the builder, the innovator. Use AI to fly and to build something new, not only to keep the job you have.

Ride the dragonOne village hid from the dragon. Another learned how it works and learned to ride it. The dragon didn't change; they did. Paddle towards the waveThe same tide knocks some people over. A prepared surfer paddles towards it and rides on top. Prepare before it arrives. Front foot, not back footDon't defend the old work. Go after new problems: build, innovate, solve, start something of your own. Be a giverTeach, help and mentor. Reach out to graduates who can't find a first job and to people losing theirs. Lift them up with you. Choose who you build forSay no to organisations that want AI only to cut jobs. Say yes to the ones using it to build something new.

Research and open standards

Clean AI Engineering

The interesting failures are almost never in the model. They are in the scaffolding around it: the context assembled wrongly, the retry that charged the customer twice, the loop that never stopped. That scaffolding can be specified, built and tested by ordinary means. These specifications are published in the open, versioned and machine-readable.

Four catalogs and two twins, for agents and for data platforms: what must exist, what must be true, and what a system must face before it meets real people or real data. Plus a lab where an agent runs for weeks while a fixer repairs it.
