The ecosystem: who does what across the lifecycle

One picture of everyone around a data project: the client, our team, and the vendors and platforms. It shows who works with whom, how each touches the data system, and in red, where the project breaks when they are not in tandem.

The picture

The whole ecosystem, in one view

Read it from top to bottom. The timeline is the ten stages of the project, with three sign-off gates. The client is on top, our team below, then the data system, then the vendors. Gray arrows are the working relationships. Each red number is a place where the link breaks.

Open the picture full width (scroll sideways on a small screen). A person icon marks each role; the letters on the body are the role tag.

In one line: a data project is a chain of hand-offs between about twenty roles in three organisations. It fails at the weakest hand-off, not at the hardest technology.

Working in tandem

Six rules that keep the links green

  1. Every link has a named pair. If you cannot name the two people on a link, it is already red.
  2. Raise outside dependencies first. Source owners, security, client IT and vendors set your pace.
  3. Review with someone who did not build it. The vendor and cloud reviews are part of the plan, not a favour.
  4. Bring operations in at MVP 1. Not at go-live.
  5. Settle definitions on real data. Show both numbers, let the owner decide, write it down.
  6. Keep the accountability. A vendor can advise. Only we sign.
