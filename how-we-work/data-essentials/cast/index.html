Topic 1 · The people

The cast: who is in a data project

A data project has more people than a software project, and most of them are outside the team. This page names all of them, in four groups, and shows what a BA, a Tech Anchor and a PM each talk to them about.

Teach first

Four groups, three seats

Most of the cast sit outside the team. The three seats are how the project talks to all of them.

In one line: if you cannot name the person in each box, that is a risk. Write the names in the stakeholder map.

Who talks to whom

The cast, and what each seat discusses with them

Decide

WhoBA talks aboutTech Anchor talks aboutPM talks about
Sponsor
Pays for it; decides go, change or stop.		Success measures; what the project is for.Rarely directly; risks that change the decision go through the PM.Weekly note; decisions; scope and date trade-offs.
Business owners
Own the question, the metric definitions and the acceptance.		Requirements, definitions, acceptance, mock-ups.Feasibility, via the BA; sample numbers.Their time; sign-offs; their escalations.
PMO and steering group
Check the project against the portfolio and the gates.		--Gate reviews; status; budget; risk.

Own the data

WhoBA talks aboutTech Anchor talks aboutPM talks about
Source system owners
Run the systems the data comes from.		What each field means; how data changes.Access method, load windows, test copy, change notice.Requests with dates; their calendar; escalation.
Data owners and stewards
Accountable for the data's quality and use.		Definitions, quality rules, who may see what.Rules in the pipeline; access design.Approvals; ownership of issues.
Finance
Own the numbers the business already trusts.		Which report to match; tolerance; sign-off.How totals are built and reconciled.Their time at month-end; budget.
Security, privacy, compliance
Protect people and the company; can stop a release.		Which data is sensitive; who may see what.Design for masking, encryption, audit.Reviews and approvals; dates.
Vendors and partners
Supply data, tools or people.		Data terms; what the vendor's data means.Interfaces, limits, support.Contract, delivery dates, SLAs.

Build and run

WhoBA talks aboutTech Anchor talks aboutPM talks about
Data engineers
Build and test the pipelines.		Clarifying requirements and meaning, daily.Design, standards, reviews, unblocking.Capacity; plan; blockers.
BI developers
Build reports and dashboards.		What each report must answer; mock-ups.Data model, performance.Plan; users' access.
Platform and cloud team
Provide environments, accounts and tooling.		-Environments, access, cost, standards.Requests with dates; cost budget.
Architects
Guard the standards across projects.		-Design reviews; exceptions.Review dates.
Test team
Test the system and the numbers.		Acceptance checks; test data.Test strategy; environments.Test plan; defects.
Operations and support
Run it after the project ends.		What users will ask; how to answer.Runbook, alerts, recovery.Hand-over; hypercare; drills.

Use

WhoBA talks aboutTech Anchor talks aboutPM talks about
End users
Use the reports and tools every day.		Their questions; training; feedback.Rarely; performance complaints.Rollout; training dates.
Analysts and data scientists
Use the data to answer questions.		What data they need and how fresh.Access, tables, performance.Onboarding; support.
Owners of downstream apps and AI
Depend on the data in other systems.		Their needs; change notice.Interfaces, contracts, freshness.Change calendar.
Regulators and auditors
Check that rules are followed.		What must be evidenced.Audit trail, retention, lineage.Audit dates; evidence.

A dash means this seat rarely talks to this person directly.
