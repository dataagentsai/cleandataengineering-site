Topic 13 · The reusable backlog

The corpus backlog for a data project

103 backlog items covering a data project from first brief to hand-over, by layer. Not tasks, not epics: things a BA, Tech Anchor or PM can say done about. Each has acceptance criteria, evidence, the causes it prevents, and when it applies. Filter it, download it, and trim it for your project.

How to use it

Start with all, remove what does not apply

Copy the whole list into your tool at the start, then remove what does not apply (the 'Applies when' note says which). Items owned by BA: 44, Tech Anchor: 37, PM: 32. Types: Document (write it down), Decision (someone decides), Spike (learn something risky, timeboxed), Slice (a thin working piece), Run (a repeated activity).

Do not skip the stage 1 to 4 items. They are cheap, and they prevent the causes that are most expensive later.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is this a Jira backlog?

No. It is a reusable starting list at the level of scope, between an epic and a task. Download the CSV, keep the items that apply, and split them into tasks in your own tool.

Why do business analysts and managers own items?

Because most of what makes a data project succeed is not engineering: owners named, definitions agreed, access raised, numbers reconciled. Those are real work and belong in the backlog.
