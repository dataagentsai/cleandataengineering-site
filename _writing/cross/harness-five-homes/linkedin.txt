"Should we build our agent on Azure or AWS?" is the second question, not the first. The harness design is the same in every home; only the product in each slot changes. One table, ten jobs, five platforms, and three questions for picking.

An agent harness has the same jobs on every platform: a model door, a loop, tools, state, durable waits, identity, guardrails, tracing, evals and hosting. Azure, Google Cloud, AWS, Databricks and open source fill those jobs with different products. Design the harness once, then pick the home by where your data and identity already live and which managed pieces your team trusts.
