We looked at 38 open-source tools for testing AI agents. 12 are in use today, 6 were turned down, and the rest wait for a reason. What we built ourselves fits in four boxes. Here is the rule we used: check if it exists, put it behind a port, and name the gap or don't build it.

AgentTwin is an assembler. Where a proven open-source tool already does a job, it plugs that tool in behind a port and builds nothing. It builds only four things no tool did: the world description format, the stand-ins that project that world into the agent's systems, the judges that read the world's state, and the switch between pretend and real. Of 38 tools we looked at, 12 are in use, 6 were turned down, and every decision has a written reason.
