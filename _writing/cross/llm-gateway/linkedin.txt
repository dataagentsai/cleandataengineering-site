If three teams each hold their own model API key, you don't have an AI platform, you have three bills and no switch. An LLM gateway is that switch. Here is what belongs in it, what doesn't, and the five mistakes we see most.

An LLM gateway is one service that sits between all your apps and every model provider. It holds the keys, the list of approved models, the limits and budgets per team, the cache, the screening and the cost log, and it switches to another provider when one fails. It is not the provider, and it is not the place for business rules about money or consent; those belong in the agent's harness.
