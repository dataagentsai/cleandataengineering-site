The app team renamed one column on a Tuesday. By Wednesday, the revenue dashboard said ₹0 and both loads said "success". A data contract would have stopped that change before it shipped. What a contract holds, where it is checked, and which changes break it, drawn simply.

Most data breakages start with an upstream change nobody announced. A data contract is a written agreement between the team that produces a dataset and the teams that use it, checked by a machine in three places. This post draws what a contract holds, where it is checked, and which changes break it.
