"Forward-deployed engineer" sounds like a job title from a spy film. It is really an engineer who sits with the customer until the thing works in their world, with their data, their rules and their cloud. Here is the job in six steps, the skills it needs, and how to grow into it.

A forward-deployed engineer works inside a customer's team to make a product or AI system actually work in their world. The job runs in six steps: discovery, a scope that does as much as possible without a model, a prototype, deploying in the customer's own cloud, proving it with evals and cost numbers, and handing over. It needs engineering plus listening, writing and judgement.
