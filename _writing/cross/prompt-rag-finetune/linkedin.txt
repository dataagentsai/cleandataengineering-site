"Let's fine-tune it on our documents" is one of the most expensive sentences in AI projects, and it usually doesn't work. Prompting fixes behaviour, RAG fixes facts, and fine-tuning fixes habits and cost at volume. Here is what each one fixes, what it costs, and three questions that tell you which one you need.

There are three ways to make a language model better at your job. Prompting changes the instructions, RAG hands it the right facts at question time, and fine-tuning trains the model itself. They fix different problems and cost very different amounts, so try them in that order and only climb when your eval set shows the step below can't do it.
