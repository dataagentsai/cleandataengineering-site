Teams building agents feel stuck. Pick a framework now and it may be old news by spring. Wait, and nothing ships. Here is our honest map of what has settled in the agent stack, what is still moving, and three rules for building anyway.

Some parts of the agent stack have settled: OpenTelemetry for traces, Postgres for state, MCP for tools, JSON Schema for typed outputs, OAuth and OpenID Connect for identity, Git and CI for releases, and open table formats for data. Others are still moving, such as frameworks, agent-to-agent protocols, memory, evals, guardrail products, GenAI field names and the models themselves. Pick boring standards for the first group, put the second behind ports with contract tests, and keep the code that protects money in your own hands.
