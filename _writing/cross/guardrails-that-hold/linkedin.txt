A note planted inside an insurance claim told our claims assistant to pay out. The model asked to do it. One small rule said no. Here are the eight things that made that rule hold, and the questions to ask of your own guardrails.

A guardrail that only exists in a prompt, or that lets everything through when it breaks, is a seatbelt drawn on your shirt. This post follows one real guardrail, "the user actually asked for this", through the eight things that make it hold: where it sits, what it reads, how it fails, how it is tested and how it is watched.
