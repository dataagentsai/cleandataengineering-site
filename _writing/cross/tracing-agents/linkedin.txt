A customer says your agent promised them money. You open the logs and find one line: the final reply. Here is how to trace an agent so you can replay the bad conversation step by step, without storing anyone's Aadhaar number.

When an agent gives a bad answer, the final reply tells you almost nothing. You need a trace: one per turn, with a span for every model call, tool call and check, carrying the model, a prompt version, tokens, cost, time and the decision, but not the customer's personal data. With that, yesterday's bad conversation becomes something you can step through and turn into a test.
