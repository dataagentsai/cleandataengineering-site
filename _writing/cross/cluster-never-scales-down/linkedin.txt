Autoscaling is a balloon with an easy pump and a sticky valve. The cluster grows at 9 a.m. for the morning load and is still big at 9 p.m., billing for workers that did nothing all afternoon. Six reasons it happens, and a fix checklist you can apply this week.

Autoscaling on Databricks grows a cluster quickly and shrinks it slowly, and sometimes not at all. The usual reasons are a minimum set too high, clusters that are never quite idle, workers holding data the autoscaler doesn't want to throw away, and streaming or shared workloads that keep everything busy. This note explains each one, the fixes, and how to see it in your own cluster events and billing tables.
