Your agent probably doesn't need Redis, a vector database, a queue, a workflow engine and a log store. It needs one PostgreSQL database with a few well-named tables. Here is the claims assistant's whole memory, table by table, and the three signs that tell you when to move something out.

An agent needs to remember a lot of different things: the conversation, what it already did, what is waiting for a person, and what it decided. One PostgreSQL database can hold all of it, which means one thing to secure, back up and pay for. Move a job out only when you see a clear sign that it has outgrown Postgres.
