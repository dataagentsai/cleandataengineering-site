Your agent said "Done, it's renewed!" Was anything renewed? A judge reading the chat can't tell. AgentTwin is a flight simulator for agents: the agent is real, the world is pretend, and the verdict comes from what changed in the world. Here is what it is, what it is not, and what is actually built.

AgentTwin keeps your AI agent real and makes everything around it pretend: the customer, the systems and data, the approver, the faults and the clock. After each scenario it judges what changed in that pretend world, not what the agent said. It is not a digital twin, a model benchmark, a load test or production monitoring, and this post is honest about which parts are built.
