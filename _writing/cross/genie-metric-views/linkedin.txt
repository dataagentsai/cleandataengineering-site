Your dashboard says ₹4.2 crore. Your new chatbot says ₹5.1 crore. The chatbot is not broken; it just wrote its own meaning of "revenue". The fix is not a better model. It is one definition, shared by chat and dashboards, drawn simply.

A chat window over your data is easy to build and easy to get wrong. The model should not write SQL against your tables; it should pick from numbers you have already defined. On Databricks that means metric views in Unity Catalog as the one definition, with AI/BI Genie and your dashboards both reading from them.
