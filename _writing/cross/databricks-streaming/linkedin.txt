"We need real-time" is the most expensive sentence in data engineering. Usually a batch every 10 minutes would do, at a fraction of the cost and the pager calls. When you really do need streaming on Databricks, here is the simple version: one medallion at two speeds, checkpoints you never delete, and a stream that only runs when there is data.

Most teams that ask for streaming need fresh data, not a stream. Start by asking how fresh the data must be; often a batch every 5 to 15 minutes is enough and far simpler. When you do stream, run the same medallion at two speeds, keep your checkpoints, set a watermark, and pick a trigger that matches how often the data actually arrives.
