Every leader is being asked the same question this year: should we build our own AI agent or buy one? The honest answer is usually "both, in the right places". Six questions, one decision card, and five things to ask your team on Monday.

Build or buy is rarely an either-or choice for an agent. Six questions decide it: how special the process is, where the data may go, what each task costs to run, what you can switch later, who owns it after launch, and what you must prove to auditors. Most teams end up in the middle: buy the plumbing, and build the rules that protect money and make you different.
