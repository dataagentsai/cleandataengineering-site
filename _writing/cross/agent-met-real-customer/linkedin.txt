Our faulty agent passed every check that reads its replies. Polite, on topic, even true. Then we looked at what it actually did in a simulated world, and it failed five out of five. Here's why scripted tests miss what real customers find, and the six kinds of scenario every agent should face.

Scripted tests send one neat question and read one neat reply. Real customers change their minds, ask two things at once, come back days later and say things in their own words, while systems time out, data goes stale, attackers plant text and approvers go quiet. We ran a good agent and a faulty one through the same five simulated scenarios: read only the replies and both pass everything; read what changed in the world and the faulty one fails all five.
