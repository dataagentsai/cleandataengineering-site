Every week brings a new agent product name, and every week someone asks "do we need this?" The question gets easy once you stop learning products and learn the fifteen jobs they do. One picture, one table, one row per job.

An autonomous agent has about fifteen jobs to do besides calling a model: the loop, the gateway, tools, context, memory, guardrails, identity, approvals, durability, tracing, evals, cost, release and more. Each job stays the same while the products doing it keep changing. This post gives one picture and one table, with two to four example tools per job across open source and the clouds.
