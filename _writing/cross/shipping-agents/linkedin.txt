Someone tidied one word out of a prompt on a Friday, straight in production, and nobody knew which version broke the refunds. An agent release is not just code: it's the prompt, the model, the tools, the checks and the config, sealed together. Here's how to ship them as one box.

An agent's behaviour comes from its prompt, its model, its tools, its checks and its settings, so release them together as one sealed, fingerprinted unit. Pin the model, gate every release on quality, cost and every model it can reach, send a small share of traffic first, rehearse the rollback, keep a kill switch, serve exactly what was tested, and stamp the release id on every trace.
