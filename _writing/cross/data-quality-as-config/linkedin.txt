Your data quality checks probably live in 40 notebooks, written 40 slightly different ways. Put the rules in one file, let one engine run them at every hop, and send bad rows to quarantine with a reason. Data quality as config, not code, drawn simply.

Most teams have data quality checks, but they are scattered across notebooks, each written a little differently. A metadata-driven framework moves every rule into a YAML file or a rules table, and one engine runs them at each hop. Bad rows go to quarantine with a reason, results land in a table, and a new dataset is a new file, not new code.
