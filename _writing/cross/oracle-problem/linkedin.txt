Your agent passed every test. Who decided it passed? If the answer is "another model read the chat and said it looked fine", you have a student marking their own paper. Here is the oracle problem in plain words, the order of checkers that solves most of it, and how to prove your checker would catch a bug.

In testing, the "oracle" is whatever decides pass or fail, and for an agent in an open conversation there is no free one. A simulation should decide with code that reads what changed in the world first, then rules on actions, then what was owed but never done, and only last an LLM judge that has been checked against people. Then test the checkers themselves by planting known bugs and seeing which ones they miss.
