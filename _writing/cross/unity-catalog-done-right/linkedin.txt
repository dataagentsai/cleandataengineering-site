Most Unity Catalog setups fail the same way: grants to named people, one giant catalog, no descriptions, and an AI agent with its own key that skips the catalog entirely. Governance for data and AI is one model, not two. Drawn simply, with five mistakes and seven questions.

Unity Catalog is the one place on Databricks that says where every table, model and agent tool lives, who may use it, and where it came from. Done right, that means a simple three-part layout, grants to groups set high up the tree, masks and filters on the table itself, and descriptions good enough for people and AI assistants to read. This post draws each part and lists the mistakes that undo it.
