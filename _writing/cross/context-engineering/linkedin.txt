Your agent's model doesn't remember anything. Before every single step, your code decides what it gets to read. Most agent mistakes and most agent bills come from getting that wrong. Five habits for what goes into the window, and what stays out.

On every step, an agent's model reads one bundle of text, called the context. What you put in it, in what order, and what you leave out decides how well the agent works and what it costs. This post shows five habits, using a real claims assistant: stable things first, fence what comes back from tools, trim, show only the allowed tools, and pass references instead of whole documents.
