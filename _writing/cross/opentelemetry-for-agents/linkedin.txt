Models, frameworks and eval tools for agents change every quarter. One choice has stopped changing: OpenTelemetry. Our claims assistant moved its traces from a laptop to Azure Application Insights by changing one line of config, and not one line of agent code.

Almost everything in an agent stack changes every few months, but how you send traces out of it has settled: OpenTelemetry. Write your spans once with its library and its standard GenAI field names, put a Collector in between, and the backend becomes one line of config. The standard is settled; some GenAI field names are still moving, so keep them in one file.
