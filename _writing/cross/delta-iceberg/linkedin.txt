Teams still lose weeks arguing Delta versus Iceberg. The bigger decision was made years ago: tables live in open files that any engine can read. Here is what a table format really does, recorded from a real Delta run, how the two compare, and how to stop choosing a side.

An open table format is Parquet files plus a log that turns a folder into a real table, with transactions, time travel, schema changes and safe concurrent writes. The move to open formats has settled. Delta Lake and Apache Iceberg do the same job with different bookkeeping, and today you can often keep one copy of the data and let readers of both kinds use it.
