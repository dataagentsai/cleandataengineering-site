Three agent-testing demos in one week, and every one looked great. That's the trap. Each simulation tool pretends to be a different part of your agent's world: the customer, the attacker, the tools, the whole environment. Here is what LangWatch, PyRIT, Strands ToolSimulator, Veris, τ²-bench and our own AgentTwin each simulate, what each leaves out, and how to pick.

Tools that test agents in a simulation each pretend to be a different part of the world. Conversation simulators such as LangWatch Scenario play the customer, red-team kits such as PyRIT play the attacker, Strands ToolSimulator plays the tools, sandboxes such as Veris play the whole environment, τ²-bench is one fixed published world, and AgentTwin builds the world from your agent's spec and judges what changed in it. Pick by the risk you fear most, and expect to combine more than one.
