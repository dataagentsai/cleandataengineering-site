Most "human in the loop" setups either ask a person about everything, or ask in a chat message the model could fake. Here is the shape that works: three risk lanes, an approval that waits for days without holding a server, and autonomy that widens only on measured agreement.

A person should approve the few actions that can't be undone, and nothing else. The approval should be a durable wait that holds no server, shows the approver enough to decide in seconds, and has a clear answer for "nobody replied". Autonomy widens only when people have agreed with the agent on that one decision, measured.
