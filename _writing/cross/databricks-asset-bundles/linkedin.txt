If your production Databricks jobs were clicked together in the UI, you can't review them, roll them back or rebuild them. Asset Bundles fix that with one folder and one YAML file. Here is the whole idea, drawn simply, from a developer's own copy to a deploy on merge.

Most Databricks jobs in production were clicked together in the workspace UI, so nobody can say what changed or rebuild them in another workspace. Databricks Asset Bundles put jobs, pipelines and their settings in one project folder, next to the code, and deploy the same folder to dev, test and prod. Add a pipeline that validates every pull request and deploys on merge as a service principal, and releases stop being a nervous afternoon.
