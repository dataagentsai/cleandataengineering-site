How do you test something that gives a different answer every time? Mostly, you don't have to. Most of an agent is plain code, and its 750 tests run in 80 seconds without calling a model once. Here is the whole testing pyramid, drawn.

Most of an agent is ordinary software, so most of its tests are ordinary tests that run in seconds for free. Tests that need a real model are few, and they sit near the top. This post draws the whole testing strategy as a pyramid, from unit tests at the bottom to production monitoring at the top.
