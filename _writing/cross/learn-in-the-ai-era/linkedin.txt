Two engineers get the same new tool and the same AI. One asks it to write the code. The other asks it to explain, then builds, then teaches. Six months later it breaks, and only one of them can fix it. Here is how to learn when answers are free: a map, a T-shape, AI as a tutor, and a loop that ends in teaching.

When AI can answer any question, facts are cheap; what's scarce is knowing what to learn, how deep to go, and proving you understand it. Draw a map of your field, learn wide at two shallow depths and deep in your core, use AI as a tutor rather than a ghostwriter, and go round the loop of see it, run it, build it, teach it.
