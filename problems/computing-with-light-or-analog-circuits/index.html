Chips, compute and energy

Computing with light or analog circuits

Follow, don't buildNeeds labs, hardware or big teams. Learn it and watch it.

Why it is still open

Photonic and analog chips promise far lower energy, but noise and precision limit them.

An experiment one person can run in 8 weeks

No solo build. Track the lab results and the first products.

Verdict: Follow, don't build

Needs fabrication.

The solo test behind the verdict

  1. Can it be measured? There is a number that shows you did better, such as an error rate, a catch rate or a cost per answer.
  2. Is it cheap to try? It runs on a laptop, free open models and open data, or under about ₹50,000 of rented GPU time.
  3. Is there a public yardstick? A benchmark or dataset others use, so your result can be compared and believed.
  4. Would a small win get noticed? A lab, a funder or a customer would care about a 10% improvement, not only a full solution.

Four yeses means Solo: strong. Two or three means Solo: possible. Fewer means Follow, don't build.
