Projects · Babu · Simulation

A day with Babuji

Babu is a companion on a tablet for older people. It speaks Hindi and English, and it remembers their life. Follow one day with Babuji, a retired teacher from Ballia, and see what Babu says and why.

This is a simulation, not the live product. The words come from Babu's conversation design, and Meera's lines are read by a computer's built-in Hindi voice, not the final voice. Babuji is made up: he is the practice person all our tests use. The memory, privacy and safety rules shown under "Inside Babu" are built and checked by automated tests. The live conversation and the tablet app are being built next.
Priya's setting for SunitaBabuji's wife died in 2019. His daughter chooses how Babu responds when he forgets. Try each one at 3:30 pm.
The internetTurn it off and see what still works. Medicine, help and goodnight never depend on it.

What's built, and what isn't

Built and testedThe memory, real deletion, the four ways of searching, the permission gate, the Sunita rules in all three settings, and working out who he means. 110 automated checks pass.
Being built nextPutting the AI's instructions together, the live AI reply, saving new memories from talk, voice in and out, then the Android tablet app.
Not in the first versionFall detection, cameras and a robot body. Babu is not a medical device and never diagnoses.
