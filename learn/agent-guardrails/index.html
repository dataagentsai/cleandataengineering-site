The agent guardrail catalog

57 common guardrails for autonomous agents, sorted by where they sit in a turn. Each one is marked must, should or could have; says whether a rule, a small classifier, an LLM judge, a person or the platform decides; and links to the test that proves it works.

35 must-haves, 19 should-haves, 3 could-haves. 39 of the 57 are plain rules in code. Only 2 need an LLM judge. The short version, with pictures, is the post Every guardrail your agent needs, sorted.

How a guardrail decides

Small classifiera small trained model gives a score

Must means every agent that can change something in the real world. Should means most agents. Could depends on the use. "Proven by" links to the matching obligation in the AI Assurance Catalog: the test that shows the guardrail really works.
