The architecture, simple then zoomed in

The whole system in five bands and two rails. Then one band opened up into five families of concerns.

First, the simple picture

Read it from the top as one request travelling down.

  1. People ask. Customers, partners and staff, on whatever channel they already use.
  2. The front door checks every request before any agent sees it: who is asking, is anything hidden in the message, is it in scope.
  3. Agents. One for each business job. Each has its own autonomy level from stop 2.
  4. Shared parts. Everything an agent needs that is not specific to its job. Built once, used by every agent.
  5. Business systems. The real records. Agents reach them only through tools, and only with the authority of the person asking.

Two rails run alongside. Lifecycle is the path every agent takes from idea to production and back round through what it learned. Governance is who owns the agent, how risky it is, and what the law requires.

Why it is drawn this way.Only the row of agents changes from one business to the next. Everything else is shared, so the second agent costs far less than the first. That is the whole argument for building a harness and not ten separate agents.

Now zoom in on the shared parts

Twelve parts is too many to hold in your head, so they are grouped into five families. Each family answers one question that a business will ask about any agent.

FamilyThe question it answersPartsWithout it
ThinkWhat does it know, how does it decide?Model gateway, Knowledge, Memory & stateThe agent guesses from what the model happens to remember
ActWhat can it do?Tool hub, Channel adaptersThe agent can talk but cannot do anything
ControlIs it safe and allowed?Identity & access, Guardrails, Human deskThe six stories in stop 5
SeeWhat did it do, what did it cost?Observability, Cost controlNobody can say what happened, or what it cost
ChangeIs it good? Can we change it safely?Eval library, Release & configEvery improvement is a gamble, so nothing gets improved

Stop 7 listed what a harness must do. Here is where each of those requirements sits in the picture.
