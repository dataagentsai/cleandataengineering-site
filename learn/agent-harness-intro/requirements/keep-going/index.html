Keep going: failures, speed and scale

What makes an agent dependable on a bad day. None of it is visible in a demo.

Requirement 15 of 33

Survive a crash

Also called: Durable execution, checkpoints

What happens to a half-finished job when a server restarts?

Why

An agent's job can last minutes, or days when it waits for approval. Servers restart far more often than that.

Without it

A restart loses the work, or repeats a payment.

Think about

  • Save after every step, not only at the end
  • On restart, skip the steps that already finished
  • How long a paused job may wait before it expires

In the big picture: Memory and state, in the Think family

Requirement 16 of 33

Do each action only once

Also called: Idempotency

What if the same step is tried twice?

Why

After a time-out, nobody knows whether the first attempt worked. Trying again must not create a second claim or a second payment.

Without it

Two claims, two payments or two emails for one request.

Think about

  • A unique key for every action
  • Tools that recognise a repeat and return the first result
  • Extra care for money and messages to customers

In the big picture: Tool hub, in the Act family

Requirement 17 of 33

Cope when something fails

Also called: Time-outs, retries, fallbacks, circuit breakers

What does the agent do when a tool or the model is down?

Why

Every system an agent touches will fail sometimes. A good agent retries a little, then stops and says so.

Without it

The agent retries all night, or invents an answer.

Think about

  • How long to wait before giving up on each call
  • How many retries, and how far apart
  • What the person is told, and who picks the work up

In the big picture: Model gateway and Human desk

Requirement 18 of 33

Be fast enough

Also called: Latency, streaming, caching

How long will a person wait, and what do they see while waiting?

Why

Each turn is a model call, and model calls are slow next to ordinary code. Five turns can feel long.

Without it

People give up before the answer arrives.

Think about

  • A time budget for the whole answer and for each turn
  • Show words as they arrive, and say what step is running
  • A smaller model for easy steps, and reuse of repeated text

In the big picture: Model gateway, in the Think family

Requirement 19 of 33

Serve many people at once

Also called: Scale, concurrency

Does it still work with a thousand conversations at the same time?

Why

If a copy of the agent keeps a conversation in its own memory, that conversation is lost when the copy goes away.

Without it

It works in the demo and fails on the first busy day.

Think about

  • All state in the shared store, none inside the agent
  • Limits from model providers are shared by all copies
  • A storm of requests should queue up and not fall over

In the big picture: Memory and state, and the front door
