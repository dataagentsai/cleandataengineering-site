FAQ

Questions people ask

How many of these problems can a tool solve?

Of 69, 55 are solved once the decision is declared, 11 are assisted (the tool suggests and checks, a person confirms) and 3 are business decisions people make, which the tool records and enforces.

Where are the hardest problems?

Silver to gold and gold to semantic: the first place several sources meet, and the place where numbers reach people, tools and agents.
