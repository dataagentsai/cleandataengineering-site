Data in motion: watch a row travel from source to dashboard

Every dot below is one row of data. Watch it leave its source, wait in a queue, land raw in bronze, get checked and cleaned in silver, and end up in the gold table behind a dashboard. Then break things on purpose: flood it, slow the sink, send bad rows, change a schema. Click any dot to follow it.

What just happened

    What each button teaches

    Each button is a real production failure, shrunk until you can watch it happen. Try them one at a time, then together.

    ButtonWhat you will seeWhat it is in real life
    Traffic spike ×4Dots arrive four times faster and the queue fills, then drains once the spike ends.A sale, a viral post or a retry storm. The queue (Kafka, Event Hubs, Kinesis, Auto Loader's file queue) is what keeps the spike from breaking the tables.
    Slow the sinkThe queue backs up until the sources are told to stop sending. Freshness gets worse and then recovers.Backpressure. A slow writer, a cold cluster or a locked table. Without a queue the rows would simply be lost.
    Bad data burstRows look fine in bronze, then turn red at the silver gate and drop into quarantine.Null keys, wrong types, broken dates. Bronze keeps everything as it arrived; silver is where checks run; quarantine keeps the bad rows so they can be fixed and replayed.
    Schema driftPartner API rows grow an extra column. Bronze accepts it as is, and silver widens its schema once.A source team adds a field without telling anyone. Schema evolution plus a rescued-data column stops the pipeline from crashing.
