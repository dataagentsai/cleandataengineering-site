The monthly bill

What the demo costs to keep running for a month, whether or not anyone talks to it. Almost everything sits inside a free tier; one database is the real bill.

About $0.73 a day running, $0.14 a day with PostgreSQL stopped. PostgreSQL left running for a whole month is about $22: more than twice the $10 budget (finding F-47). So it is stopped when idle, and Azure restarts a stopped server after 7 days.

Why not one server always on? One always-on replica would leave the free grant (about $1.20 a day). The price of scaling to zero: a cold start of up to 55 seconds after idle (flow 10), and DBOS timers that only fire when a replica wakes.

The $10 budget emails the owner at 50% and 80% of actual spend and at a 100% forecast. It never switches anything off, and cost data arrives hours late.
