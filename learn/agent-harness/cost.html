Agent Harness Tutorial · Cost

Cost: where the money goes

An agent costs money in two ways. Every turn spends a little on the model (tokens in and out), and the platform costs a fixed amount every month whether anyone talks to it or not. The harness measures the first per turn, caps it with budgets, and keeps the second small.

Goes inA turn, and a month of running
What happensPrice every model call as it happens; stop a turn that gets too dear; watch the monthly bill
Comes outA cost per turn you can see in the trace, and no surprises on the invoice

The two numbers to remember. An agentic turn on the demo costs well under a tenth of a cent: between $0.00014 and $0.00093 in the real flows. A direct, refused or blocked turn costs $0 in model tokens. The hard ceiling per turn is $0.50, about 500 times what a real turn has used.
