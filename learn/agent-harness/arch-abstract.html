Agent Harness Tutorial · Architecture

The abstract architecture: the big picture

The system without any vendor names: vertical agents on shared horizontal assets. Vertical agents do the business. Horizontal assets make every vertical cheaper.

Goes inA request from someone who asks
What happensIt passes a channel and the front door, is routed to one vertical agent, which uses the shared assets to reach the systems of record
Comes outThe next agent, or the next group company, starts from the same platform instead of from zero

Seven bands, top to bottom. Who asks, where they ask, the front door every request passes, orchestration (the router, hand-offs between agents, a registry of agents), the vertical agents (one per business job, each with its autonomy level), the horizontal assets every agent reuses, and the systems of record the tools read and write with the user's authority.

Two rails. On the left, the lifecycle every agent goes through, from "is AI the right fix?" to "every miss becomes a test". On the right, governance: a named owner, a risk tier, an autonomy level, an audit trail, regulation (IRDAI, the DPDP Act) and an AI council.

The vertical-agents band changes per business. Everything else is shared.

Want every detail? The long version is in the Series: the abstract architecture.
