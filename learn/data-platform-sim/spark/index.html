DataTwin Studio · Spark internals

Spark internals, recorded

What Spark actually does with your code. Each step shows the code, the result, every stage with its tasks as a timeline (so you can see waves and the one slow task), the query plan, the jobs and the logs. Seven scenarios on 2 million orders, from 'an action becomes tasks' to 'AQE splits a skewed join'.

real Recorded on local[2] Spark 3.5.1 on 2026-10-27. Every stage, task, byte shuffled, plan and timing comes from Spark's own event log and EXPLAIN. Timings are from a laptop with 2 cores: compare them with each other, not with a cluster.

Arrow keys work too. Click a step, a tab or a commit to look closer.
