What really happens on disk when you create, insert, update and delete in a Delta table. Six scenarios, from 'a table is files plus a log' to 'why VACUUM breaks time travel'.

real Recorded on local[2] Spark 3.5.1 + Delta Lake 3.2.0 on 2026-10-27. Nothing here is drawn by hand: the files, commits, errors and timings are what Delta actually wrote and reported.
