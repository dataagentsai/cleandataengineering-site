DataTwin Studio · Delta Lake internals

Delta Lake internals, recorded

What really happens on disk when you create, insert, update and delete in a Delta table. Each step shows the command, the table's rows, every Parquet file on disk (live, or kept only for time travel), the commit files in _delta_log, the table history and the Spark jobs. Six scenarios, from 'a table is files plus a log' to 'why VACUUM breaks time travel'.

real Recorded on local[2] Spark 3.5.1 + Delta Lake 3.2.0 on 2026-10-28. Nothing here is drawn by hand: the files, commits, errors and timings are what Delta actually wrote and reported.

Arrow keys work too. Click a step, a tab or a commit to look closer.
