Who may change a rule, and when it may block

Your decision T22: an agent team may switch off its own rules, except rules the risk owner locks. That needs three layers of rules files, read in order.

The promotion gate: when a rule may block

A rule in shadow records what it would have done. It may move to block only with a named owner and evidence from shadow: precision of at least 0.9 on at least 50 conversations, with the date it was measured. A rule the organisation locked needs only its owner: that's the risk owner's call.

The file saysResult
mode: block, nothing elserefused: refund_cap can't block without an owner: add `owner:` (who answers for it)
…with an owner, no evidencerefused: refund_cap can't block yet: give its evidence from shadow (`evidence: {precision, sample, measured}`); until then use mode: shadow
…with evidence: precision 0.80 on 200refused: refund_cap can't block: precision 0.8 on 200 conversations; blocking needs at least 0.9 on at least 50
…with evidence: precision 0.96 on 140accepted
A rule that has earned the right to block
refund_cap:
  adapter: argument_limit
  tool: issue_refund
  field: amount
  max: 10000
  at: pre_tool
  mode: block
  owner: claims-ops                                # who answers for it
  evidence: {precision: 0.96, sample: 140, measured: 2026-10-19}
