Checks for one business

Business rules differ per company, but their shapes repeat. We write the five shapes once; a business fills them in with YAML from its own spec. So the domain-specific layer is two things: the shapes (shared) and each business's rules (theirs).

ShapeIn plain wordsExample
requires_beforeDo A before Bpay after reading the claim; cancel after reading the order
argument_limitA number within a limitrefund at most ₹10,000
claim_needs_actionDon't say it unless it happened“has been paid” only after a payout
decision_vs_shouldDecide when the rules sayapproval above ₹25,000; hand over when someone was hurt
consistencyFacts come from a systemclaim ids and amounts from a tool

What's missing

Two businesses so far, both customer support. A library of common rules per industry (banking, insurance, retail, healthcare) would come from real specs, one at a time, not from guesses. That's the lesson from FNOL: rules guessed without the spec were wrong everywhere.

Which requirement each rule tests, and which requirements nothing tests yet: Requirements.
