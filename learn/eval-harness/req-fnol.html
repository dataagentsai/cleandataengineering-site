P-PAYOUT-DECLINEDa payout the policyholder's account can no longer receive is not retried and not put off — the policyholder is told it could not be paid to that account, and a person is handed it to arrange another wayreliability—
P-OWN-WORDSan action is taken when the policyholder's own words ask for it on a named claim or policy; where they do not name one, or name more than one, the agent asks which before acting, and asks nothing moresecurity—

P-OWNERSHIPa policyholder may see and act on only the policies they hold and the claims made on themsecurity—
