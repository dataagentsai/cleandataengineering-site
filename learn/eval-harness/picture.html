The whole picture, one step at a time

Why the steps are separate. Each step meets the next through a fixed shape (a port). A Phoenix reader and an App Insights reader both produce the same Session, so the checks never know where the data came from. A Presidio check and one of our patterns both answer with the same result, so the reports never know who judged. Swapping a tool means adding one adapter and changing one line of YAML.

Why “decide” is its own step. A check only says what it found. Whether that blocks a reply, raises an alert or is just counted is a separate choice, so the same check can block in one company and only alert in another.

We never replace a team's tools. No database, dashboard or chat UI of our own: we read from where traces already are and write verdicts back there.
