Names and numbers to check.
  • The certification web page lists ten sections and shows Data Sharing and Federation as its own 5%. The 9 October guide has nine sections and puts sharing, Clean Rooms and federation under Section 2 (12%). This site follows the guide. Check both pages two weeks before you sit.
  • Names in the guide: Lakeflow Pipelines and Lakeflow Jobs; AUTO CDC (formerly APPLY CHANGES); Declarative Automation Bundles (formerly Databricks Asset Bundles); Git folders (formerly Repos); OpenSharing for Delta Sharing's D2D and Databricks-to-Open modes. Docs and older courses may use the former names.
  • The guide's own AI Prep Guide warns that chatbots give outdated answers on Databricks. Check any fact you are unsure about on docs.databricks.com.

About the questions: every practice item is original, written to the guide's objectives and checked against docs.databricks.com. None is a real exam question and none reproduces the guide's sample questions. Each has a deliberate runner-up (a good practice that wins if one fact changes), and every wrong option says why it is wrong.
How hard is Mock #1? Be honest with yourself. Checks run on the 60 items: the correct option is the longest in about 30% of items and the shortest in about 17% (chance is 25%), and answers are spread evenly across A to D. Three independent reviewers solved the first version blind and scored 60 of 60, with top confidence on 48. Six items they flagged were rewritten. So this is a learning paper: the choices are real, but it is easier than the exam is likely to feel. A high score here is a floor, not proof you are ready. Use the walkthrough tables and the traps column for the harder reading practice.
