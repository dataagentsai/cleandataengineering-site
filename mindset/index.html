Mindset · for anyone scared or confused about AI

Train the dragon

If you are in college, just graduated, have lost your job, or lie awake worried you might: this is for you. It starts with a story.

The dragon

News spread through the valley. A dragon had come, huge, with fire pouring from its mouth. Wherever it flew, villages burned. Fields, houses, everything in front of it.

Village after village heard the news and was afraid. Some hid. Some built higher walls. Some packed up and waited for the worst.

The village that decided

But one village made a different decision. They didn't ask, "how do we hide from it?" They asked, "what if we learned how this creature works?"

They watched it. They studied what it ate, where it rested, what made it angry and what made it calm. They practised, failed, and practised again.

And then they flew

One day the dragon came again, and this time it didn't burn the village. The villagers had learned to ride it.

The same fire that destroyed the other villages now cleared their land, carried their goods over mountains, and let them see further than anyone in the valley had ever seen. The dragon didn't change. They did.

AI is the dragon. It is real, and it is powerful. It will change many jobs, and some people will be hurt; pretending otherwise would be dishonest. But all around you, people are learning it in a particular way: building things, creating things, solving problems, and letting AI help them fly. The difference between the villages was never the dragon. It was the decision.

The lion mindset

The dragon, the surfer and the lion all teach the same thing. The best way to survive is not to try to survive. A lion doesn't spend the day hiding from the jungle; it moves first.

Be the driver, the builder, the innovator. Use AI to fly, and to build something that didn't exist before.

Front foot, not back footDon't defend the old work. Go after new problems, and let AI do what used to need a team.
Be a giverTeach, help and mentor. Reach out to graduates who can't find a first job and to people losing theirs. Lift them with you.
Choose who you build forSay no to organisations that want AI only to cut jobs. Say yes to the ones using it to build something new.
Ride, don't hideLearn how the dragon works, paddle towards the wave, and fly.
