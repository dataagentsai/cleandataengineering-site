Mindset · Your first 30 days

Your first 30 days

Fear shrinks when you start moving. Here is a month that turns "I'm worried about AI" into "I built something with AI, and I know what to learn next".

Where are you starting from?

Still in collegeYou have the most time and the least to lose. Build in public now; by graduation, your projects speak louder than your marks.
Just graduatedDon't wait for the perfect job to teach you. Join a real project, ship something, and you will interview with evidence instead of hope.
Lost your jobFirst, breathe: it is not a verdict on you. Your domain experience is gold. Pair it with AI and you can solve problems in your field that a fresh graduate can't even see.
Scared of losing itYour job is your laboratory. Find the work that repeats in your team, solve it with AI, and become the person who brings the dragon home.

Four weeks, one step each

Week 1 · Draw the mapRead the AI Universe. Pick the one family closest to your work. Learn its concepts, not product names.
Week 2 · Build something tinyMake one small thing that works: a script, a chatbot over your notes, an agent that does one job. It doesn't have to be good. It has to run.
Week 3 · Join something realPick a project that is under way and take a good first task. Real code, real reviews, real people.
Week 4 · Share and askWrite what you learned in five minutes of reading. Then talk to five people about a problem they have every week. That conversation is your next project.
One rule for the whole month: learn the concept, then build it once. A product name on a CV fades in a year. Understanding why a system works, and having built one, lasts a career.
